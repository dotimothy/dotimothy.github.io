About TheDoShoots

Hello! I'm Timothy, a passionate photographer running TheDoShoots. My journey into photography began several years ago, when my father used to capture memories of our family with his collections of cameras. Now, having earned my Eagle Scout rank and being especially fond of the Photography Merit Badge, I have embraced photography as a way to express my creativity and share the beauty I see in the world.

This website serves as a curated collection of my work, a glimpse into the worlds I explore through my lens. Thank you for visiting and sharing in my passion!
