👋 About TheDoShoots 👋

Hello! I'm Timothy, a passionate photographer running TheDoShoots. My journey into photography began several years ago, when my dad used to capture memories of our family with his collections of cameras. Fastforward to now, graduating from UCLA with a M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, and earning my Eagle Scout rank being especially fond of the Photography Merit Badge, I have embraced photography as a way to express my creativity and appreciation of Mother Nature.

This portfolio serves as a curated collection of my work, a glimpse into the worlds I explore through my various lenses. Thank you for visiting and sharing in my passion!
