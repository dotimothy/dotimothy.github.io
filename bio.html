Timothy Do's Biography

Timothy Do is from San Jose, California graduating Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering, double specializing in Digital Signal Processing and Communications, from the University of California, Irvine. He will pursue a Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering, specializing in Signal Processing, at the University of California, Los Angeles. Timothy has initiated into the Tau Beta Pi Engineering Honor Society in the Fall of 2023, in addition to the IEEE Eta Kappa Nu (HKN) Honor Society in the same period. Timothy continues to prove his dedication to the engineering community through his involvement in these organizations in addition to his volunteerism to the Boy Scouts of America as a Merit Badge Counselor. His most fond memories at UC Irvine were leading the Institute of Printed Circuits Club (IPC) as its Vice President, researching wildfire hyperspectral imagery for the Irvine Computer Vision Laboratory (ICVL) under Professor Glenn Healey, and collecting over 25+ Petr Stickers while zipping on his electric scooter during his undergraduate studies.
