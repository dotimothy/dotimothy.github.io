Introduction

I am fourth year Electrical Engineering Undergraduate at UC Irvine, specializing in Digital Signal Processing. To accelerate my studies, I have vehemently taken a graduate course in Digital Image Processing and am apart of the Accelerated Status program for a Master’s Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

I am currently an undergraduate student researcher for the Irvine Computer Vision Laboratory under the direction of Professor Glenn Healey. My research interests include Hyperspectral Image Processing, Embedded IoT Systems, and Parallel GPU Optimizations with Neural Networks. In the industry, I was an Industrial IoT Intern for Western Digital during the Summer of 2022.
