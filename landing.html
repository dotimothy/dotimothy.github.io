Introduction

I am fourth year Electrical Engineering Undergraduate at UC Irvine,double specializing in Digital Signal Processing and Communications. To accelerate my studies, I have vehemently taken graduate courses in Digital Image Processing and Hardware Accelerators.. I am apart of the Accelerated Status program to work towards a Master’s of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

I am currently an undergraduate student researcher for the Irvine Computer Vision Laboratory under the direction of Professor Glenn Healey. We are currently researching on Spectral Image Filtering on Wildfire Scenes. My other research interests include Hyperspectral Image Processing, Embedded IoT Systems, and Parallel GPU Optimizations with Neural Networks.

Experience

I have electrical design experience in Oscilloscopes, Spectrum Analyzers, and Multimeters. I also have programming assets such as MATLAB for Image Processing, Python for Data Analytics/Tensorflow AI, Java for Android App development, and C for Embedded IoT Systems. I recently led a team of 4 to complete my Senior Design Project "The Do-Pro: A Simplistic Stereo-Vision Camera"!

In the industry, I was an Industrial IoT Intern for Western Digital during the Summer of 2022, where I got certified as a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt for learning about business practices for a technical coporation. I applied Six Sigma methodologies and other industrial toolsets to automate data collection for a sensor.
