About Me

I’m a Senior Developer Evangelist at SiMa.ai, where I bring Physical AI and generative AI to the Modalix platform and help developers build on it. I earned an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UCLA, specializing in signal processing, and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from UC Irvine, where I graduated Magna Cum Laude. During undergrad I took graduate courses in digital image processing and in languages and compilers for hardware accelerators through the Accelerated Status program.

At UC Irvine I led undergraduate research at the Irvine Computer Vision Laboratory under Professor Glenn Healey, studying spectral image analysis of wildfire scenes. My other research interests include hyperspectral image processing, embedded IoT systems and parallel GPU optimization for neural networks.
